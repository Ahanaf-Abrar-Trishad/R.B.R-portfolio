## Design Context

### Users
International organisations and funders (FAO, WUR, NGOs, grant bodies) and employers / research labs (PIs hiring research assistants or scientists). They arrive from a CV, email or conference contact, skim for credibility in under a minute, then dig into research, publications and impact evidence (certificates). Desktop first for committee reading, phone for conference follow-ups.

### Brand Personality
Rigorous, warm, driven. A serious early-career aquatic ecotoxicologist (microplastics) who also leads an all-women community innovation team. Credible first, personality second. Should evoke confidence and trust, with a spark of energy.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light, editorial theme (owner switched from dark after critique, 2026-11-17): paper-like tinted neutrals, river/ecology hue, one warm accent used rarely, varied section layouts instead of cards. Sakuga VFX (anime impact effects) kept but used sparingly, only on the primary hero CTA and the certificate tabs, so they surprise rather than dominate.
Section order stays: Hero → Education & Awards → Research → Publications → Leadership → Conferences → Certificates → Contact.
Anti-references: generic AI/tech template (dark + neon, identical card grids, startup landing), stiff academic CV page (walls of text), childish or gimmicky.

### Design Principles
1. Evidence over decoration: real numbers, real certificates and real places do the persuading.
2. Scannable in 60 seconds: hierarchy must surface degree, research focus, top awards and contact without scrolling hunts.
3. Restraint with motion: one or two memorable moments, everything else calm.
4. Warmth through content and people (community impact, team, places), not through effects.
5. Avoid template sameness: vary section layouts, not card-after-card.
